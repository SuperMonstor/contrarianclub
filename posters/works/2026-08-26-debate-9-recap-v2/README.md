# Debate #9, recapped (feed cut)

Ten slide recut of `2026-08-23-debate-9-recap`, restructured for engagement on
the feed rather than for the record. Same night, same numbers, same art.

## What changed from the twelve slide cut

- **The cover leads with the paradox**, not the event's name: "Minds moved.
  The needle did not." The precise claim sits under it, scoped to the opening
  motion so the shout stays true, and the second motion is teased as a second
  hook.
- **One chart per motion instead of two.** Before is a ghost bar, after is
  gold, both scaled against the tallest bar of either round. The first cut
  normalised each round to itself on separate slides, which compared honestly
  only if you swiped back and forth.
- **The full-scale traveller is promoted** from a caption into the gold pull
  line of the first swing slide.
- **A mid-deck tease** at the foot of the first swing slide ("Motion two did
  move") bridges the flat half of the night to the dramatic half.
- **The close asks for a comment**: score yourself on motion one, minus three
  to plus three, on the same scale the deck has spent nine slides teaching.
  Put the motion text in the post caption too, so commenters do not have to
  swipe back.
- Dropped: nothing of substance. The verdict, odds and ends, and both motion
  slides carry over unchanged.

## Where the numbers come from

All figures are the 2026-08-23 deck's figures, read from the live poll for
event `8SRKJQ` in Supabase. **See that work's README for the full provenance,
populations, and the checked facts on the motion slides.** The same caveats
apply: chart slides count everyone who voted in that round, swing slides count
only the devices that voted in both, and each slide names its population.

## Suggested post caption

Open the caption with the verdict line ("The arguments did not move the
divided room. They moved the certain one."), then the two motions in full,
then the ask: score yourself on motion one, -3 to +3, in the comments.
