# Debate Club #12, government schools carousel

The carousel for the motion:

> This Club Would end government-run schooling

The government still pays where it must and still sets and enforces
standards, but owns and runs no school. Aided schools are already privately
run and are not included. The clash: despite its dire state, is there enough
merit in the system to be worth fixing, or does it need tearing down and
rebuilding?

## Open questions

- **The guest (slide 8).** Not named. A name and role would make the slide
  far more credible. If he is from CJP, slide 2 and the hook can say so.
- **"A first of its kind in Bangalore."** The club's own claim, and not one
  anyone has checked. "A first for the club" is the safe version.
- **Slide 1's photograph** is an empty classroom whose file does not say
  where it was taken. The credit names no place for that reason. Swap it for
  an Indian government school if one turns up.
