# Debate Club #12

Theme only. The motions are held back until the room, as at #11. Date,
venue and tickets are left off the poster on purpose.

## The theme

**The Indian education system.** It is printed in the club's own words as
the headline. Earlier cuts carried two themes (government schools, and
relationships and the law) and before that one question over both ("Where
does the state belong?"). The question read as a slogan, and the
relationships theme was dropped from the night.

## Description

> Debate Club #12 is coming up, and this time we're taking on the Indian
> education system.
>
> We don't share the exact motions until the day. That way nobody shows up
> with a speech ready, and if it's your first time, you're on the same
> footing as everyone else.
>
> **How it works**
>
> The format changes a bit from event to event, but a couple of things stay
> the same. We run a raffle and pull speakers from the audience to come up
> and debate. And there's always an open floor section, where anyone in the
> room can jump in.
>
> **What to expect**
>
> Expect to hear opinions you don't agree with, and to have people push back
> on yours. You don't have to speak if you don't want to. Lots of people just
> come to listen, and that's fine.
>
> **How to join the club**
>
> Come to the debates and speak. If you do a great job, or you keep showing
> up, you'll get an invite.
