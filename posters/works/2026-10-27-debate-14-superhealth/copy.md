# Debate Club #14, with Superhealth

Eakins's Gross Clinic, the motion as the title, and the guest who argues
against it, with his Apollo past. The club's lockup and Superhealth's wordmark share the top.
Laid out as #11 to #13 were. Date, venue and tickets are left off, as on
those three.

Three formats: `ig-portrait` for the feed, `ig-story`, and `ig-square` for
WhatsApp, where a portrait image is shown narrow on desktop. The square
roughly doubles its width there and still reads on phones.

## The title

The motion itself, so it is printed once rather than as a question and then
again underneath. Earlier cuts led with a question ("Should hospitals be
allowed to make a profit?", then "Should hospitals profit from your
sickness?", then "Can for-profit hospitals ever put patients first?"); the
last of those is still the carousel's cover and close.

## The motion

The club wrote it as:

> TCBT for profit hospitals can never put patients first

Printed with TCBT spelled out, as on every published motion, and with the
hyphen in "for-profit":

> This Club Believes that for-profit hospitals can never put patients first.

Set as the title: "This Club Believes that" small on one line, then the claim
in three big lines broken at phrase boundaries: "for-profit hospitals / can
never put / patients first."

## The guest

Varun Dubey, founder and CEO of Superhealth, argues against the motion: that
profiting off healthcare is still good for patients. The poster sets him in
ivory Playfair, larger than the series' blocks: "Varun Dubey, CEO of
Superhealth, / formerly Chief Revenue Officer / at Apollo Hospitals." A gold
name was tried and dropped. Before Superhealth he was Chief Revenue Officer
at Apollo Hospitals and CMO at Ola Electric
([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/industry-briefing-news/former-apollo-cro-ola-electric-cmo-varun-dubey-launches-superhealth-hospitals-145294.html)).
