# Debate Club #12, world-class universities carousel

The carousel for the motion:

> This Club Believes that India's prevailing attitudes towards education are
> too deeply entrenched to produce world-class universities in the near future

## What the motion means by "attitudes"

Not "Indians have a bad mentality". The beliefs and incentives around
education: college as a route to a job rather than to ideas, marks and ranks
over learning, safe careers over research, low tolerance for failure, family
pressure, status, and the low standing of academics. The clash for the room
is whether Indian universities fall short because of institutional problems,
or because the society around them wants something else from them. Slides 4
and 5 give each side its own slide.
